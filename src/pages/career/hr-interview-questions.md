HR rounds test how clearly you communicate and whether you are honest and ready to learn. Prepare your own answers, and do not memorise someone else's.

## 1. Tell me about yourself
Use three parts: who you are now (course and college), what you have done (skills and projects), and what you want next. Keep it under one minute.

## 2. Why should we hire you?
Match one or two of your real skills to the job, and give one example from a project.

## 3. What are your strengths?
Choose two strengths that are true and give a short example for each.

## 4. What is your weakness?
Name a real, small weakness and say what you are doing to improve it. Avoid fake weaknesses such as "I work too hard".

## 5. Why do you want to work here?
Read the company's website and the job post first. Mention something specific about their work.

## 6. Where do you see yourself in 5 years?
Talk about learning and growing in the role, not about a specific title.

## 7. Tell me about a project you are proud of
Explain the problem, what you built, the tools you used, and what you learned.

## 8. How do you handle pressure or deadlines?
Give a real example, such as an exam week or a project deadline, and what you did.

## 9. Do you have any questions for us?
Prepare two, such as how the team trains new joiners or what a good first three months looks like.

## 10. Are you willing to relocate or work in shifts?
Answer honestly. If you have conditions, say so politely.

## Before the interview
- Know every skill on your resume well enough to explain it.
- Practise your answers aloud once.
- Keep your resume copy and a pen ready.
