A clear LinkedIn profile helps recruiters find you and understand what you do.

## 1. Profile photo
A clear, well-lit photo of your face, with a plain background. No group photos.

## 2. Headline
Do not leave it as just "Student". Use your course, your skills and your goal.

Example: `B.Tech CSE Student | Python and SQL | Looking for data internships`

## 3. About section
Write 3 to 5 short lines:
1. Who you are and what you study
2. Your main skills and what you have built
3. What you are looking for now

## 4. Education
Add your college, course, years and CGPA if it is strong.

## 5. Projects
Add each project with a one-line description, the tools used and a link (GitHub or demo).

## 6. Skills
Add 10 to 15 real skills that match the roles you want. Ask classmates and teachers who know your work to endorse them.

## 7. Connections and activity
- Connect with classmates, teachers, seniors and people in roles you want.
- Send a short personal note with each request.
- Share what you learn: a project, a course you finished, a short lesson.

## 8. Match it to your resume
The role, dates, skills and projects on LinkedIn should match your resume.

## Quick checklist
- [ ] Clear photo
- [ ] Headline with skills and goal
- [ ] About section written
- [ ] 2 or more projects with links
- [ ] 10 or more skills
- [ ] 50 or more relevant connections
